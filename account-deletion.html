Delete Your Unbound Account

You can permanently delete your Unbound account and the data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Unbound and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Privacy.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Enter your current password when prompted and confirm the deletion.

When account deletion completes, the Unbound backend deletes your account and associated Community data. The app then removes its local data from the device.

If you cannot access the app

Email unbound.breakfree@gmail.com and request deletion of your Unbound account. We may ask for information sufficient to verify and identify the account. Do not send your password.

What deletion cannot remove

Deletion removes data stored by Unbound. It cannot remove copies you have independently made outside the app, such as screenshots, copied text or other manually saved information.

For more information about how Unbound handles information, see the Privacy Policy.
