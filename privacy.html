Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unbound handles information when you use the Unbound recovery app. Unbound is developed by James in the United Kingdom.

Information we process

Account information. If you create an account, your email address and authentication information are processed to create and authenticate your account. Authentication is provided through Supabase.

Community information. If you choose to use the optional Community features, Unbound processes the information you choose to provide there, including a display name, a preset avatar, an optional bio, posts, comments, reactions, reports and blocks. Community content is sent to and stored by the app's Supabase backend so that the Community can function.

Recovery information stored on your device. Your personal recovery information is designed to remain on your device rather than being uploaded to our server. This includes information such as your sobriety start date/time, check-ins, cravings, goals, journal entries, recovery motivations and reasons, triggers, recovery statement, progress/XP, achievements, celebrations and notification preferences. This local app database is encrypted using SQLCipher, with its encryption key protected using the device's secure storage.

You may voluntarily include personal or sensitive information in Community content. Do not post information you do not want other Community users to see.

Notifications

Unbound uses local device notifications for optional reminders such as check-ins, encouragement, goals and milestones. Unbound does not use push notifications for Community activity and does not require an Expo push token for these reminders.

What we do not use

Unbound does not contain advertising, advertising profiles, analytics tracking, subscriptions or in-app payments. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Supabase is used for account authentication and the optional Community functionality. Information required for those services is transmitted over HTTPS and may be processed on infrastructure operated by Supabase and its service providers.

How we use information

Information is used to provide account authentication, operate the optional Community, provide the recovery features on your device, maintain security, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Security

Unbound uses reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled. Data transmitted to the backend is sent over HTTPS. Personal recovery data stored locally is protected by encrypted local storage. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your Unbound account from within the app by going to Settings → Privacy → Delete account and completing the confirmation process. Account deletion removes the account and associated Community data held by the Unbound backend, and the app removes its local data after successful account deletion.

If you cannot access the app, you can contact unbound.breakfree@gmail.com to request account deletion. We may need information sufficient to verify and identify the account. Never send your password by email.

Deletion cannot remove information that you have independently saved outside Unbound, such as screenshots or copied text. Information may also be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate security, abuse-prevention or moderation purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. To exercise an applicable right, contact unbound.breakfree@gmail.com.

Children

Unbound is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact unbound.breakfree@gmail.com.

Unbound is a recovery support app and is not a substitute for professional medical, mental-health or emergency services.
